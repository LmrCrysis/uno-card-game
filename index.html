UNO rules + Draw Two stacking: 2–10 players; deal seven cards each. Match color, number, or symbol. You may play a matching card or draw a card; if the drawn card is playable, you may play that card this turn. Skip skips the next player; Reverse changes direction; Draw Two makes the next player draw two and miss their turn. Wild chooses a color. Wild Draw Four is legal only if you have no card of the current color, and it cannot be challenged. Draw Two cards can be stacked on any color +2, and Wild Draw Four cards can be stacked on +4: each matching stack adds its penalty to the total, which passes onward until someone draws the full total and loses their turn. Call UNO when you play your second-to-last card: on PC, press Space to call UNO or catch a missed UNO; on mobile, tap the matching on-screen button. If caught before the next player begins, draw two. After each round ends, reveal all remaining hands. First out scores the value of opponents’ remaining cards; first to 300 wins.
